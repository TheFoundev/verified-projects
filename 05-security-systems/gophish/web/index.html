A phishing drill, counted as it happens.

Phishdrill plans a campaign: every target gets a send record with a random tracking id. Opens, clicks and reports are recorded on the sends, and Summary computes the funnel of a campaign: the sends, the opens, the clicks, the reports, the open and the click rate over the sends and the report rate over the opens. No mail is sent, the delivery is simulated.

Architecture

How it works

campaign.go holds the model. Campaign has a name, a template and a map of the sends, keyed by the tracking id. Plan creates a Send for every target that is not empty; token returns eight random bytes in hexadecimal and falls back to a counter when the random source fails, so two sends never share a tracking id. RecordOpen sets the flag of a send, RecordClick sets the opened and the clicked flag, and RecordReport sets the reported flag; mark looks the send up in the map and returns false when the tracking id is unknown. Summary counts the flags and computes the open and the click rate over the sends and the report rate over the opens. main.go takes the campaign, the template and the targets from the command line and records a plausible funnel: the first mail is opened, the second one is clicked and reported.
