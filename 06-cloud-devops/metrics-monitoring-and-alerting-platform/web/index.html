The value of a metric, and the rule that watches it.

Beacon keeps the samples of a metric in memory. Rate, Sum and Average read the samples of a window, and a rule with a threshold and a for-duration fires when the value of that window is above the threshold. The samples arrive as JSON on /ingest, the store answers a query on /query, and the counters are also written in the text format Prometheus reads.

What it does

The capabilities

Every panel below is served by the real API of this project — nothing here is a mock-up.

⌗

Samples with labels

A sample has a metric, a map of labels, a value and the time it was taken.

◑

Three windows

Rate, Sum and Average read the samples of a metric that lie in a window.

⚖

Rules with a for-duration

A rule fires when the value of its window is above the threshold.

◷

Alerts with a time

An alert keeps the value, the threshold and the time of the first sample of the window.

▤

Prometheus text

The store writes the sample counter and the rate of every metric in the text format Prometheus reads.

⇄

Two handlers

IngestHandler takes a JSON list of samples and QueryHandler answers the rate and the average of a metric.

Architecture

How it works

metrics.go holds the model and the store. Sample carries a metric, its labels, its value and its time; Store keeps the samples in a slice behind a sync.RWMutex and Add puts them there. windowLocked and window pick the samples of one metric that lie in a window and sort them by time, Rate divides the difference of the last and the first value by the time between them, Sum adds the values of the window and Average divides that sum by the number of samples. Rule carries a threshold, a for-duration and a mode; Evaluate averages the metric over the for-duration of the rule, takes the rate instead when the mode is rate, and writes an Alert with the value, the threshold and the time of the first sample of the window when the value is above the threshold. Store.Prometheus writes the sample counter and the rate of every metric in the text format Prometheus reads, and IngestHandler, QueryHandler and PrometheusHandler serve the store over HTTP. main.go fills the store with the sample series of two metrics and with one rule, and prints the rate, the average and the alerts.

The API

Endpoints

MethodPathWhat it does
GET/api/summaryThe samples, the metrics, the rules and the alerts of the store
GET/api/metricsEvery metric with its samples, its rate, its sum and its average
GET/api/samplesEvery sample of the store with its labels and its time
GET/api/rulesThe alert rules with their threshold and their for-duration
GET/api/alertsThe alerts of the last evaluation
GET/api/prometheusThe counters in the text format Prometheus reads
POST/api/ingestAdd samples to the store and answer with the number of samples
POST/api/queryThe rate, the sum and the average of one metric in a window
